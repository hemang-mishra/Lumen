### Personal Knowledge Decay

Humans accumulate experiential wisdom throughout life, but unlike factual knowledge, personal wisdom is highly context-dependent and decays when the original context disappears.

For example:

- When struggling with productivity, I learn that flow and intrinsic motivation matter more than discipline.
- When dealing with insecurity, I learn that self-worth tied to outcomes creates suffering.
- When overwhelmed by difficult tasks, I learn that breaking problems into smaller pieces reduces anxiety.

These lessons may be transformative in the moment, yet years later I face similar situations and fail to recall them when they are most needed.

The challenge is not acquiring wisdom.

The challenge is retaining and retrieving wisdom at the right moment.
