Level 1: Facts
I studied at X college.
I interned at Y company.
My weight is 63 kg.

These are easy to store.

Level 2: Experiences
During my internship I felt overwhelmed.
I had a conflict with a friend.
I was anxious about placements.

Harder, but still storable.

Level 3: Wisdom

This is what you're really after.

Examples:

"When overwhelmed, break the problem into smaller pieces."
"Perfectionism matters less than consistency."
"Comparing gym numbers destroys motivation."
"The work I do today should not define my identity."

These are distilled lessons.

This is the valuable stuff.
